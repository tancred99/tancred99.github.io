About and settings

Word hints under each sentence give the meaning of less common words. Tap Show translation for the whole sentence; it also appears after you answer.

Typed answers ignore capital letters, extra spaces, and ё/е differences. A missed item comes back 3–6 questions later and has to be answered correctly before the session ends.

When you're signed in, your lesson progress, drill scores and My words are saved to your account, so they follow you to any phone or computer. Without an account they stay in this browser only. Export makes a backup file of My words.

To add grammar exercises, edit exercises.json; starter vocabulary lives in vocab.json, and lessons in lessons.json. The page loads them when hosted and falls back to its built-in copy when you open the file directly.
